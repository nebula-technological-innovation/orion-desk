How to stand this up without lying about it.

The app is a desk. A live pool is a treasury plus stratum. A no-KYC convert is a router, not a book.

1. Stratum vendors

Do not write a stratum server. Buy or fork something that already speaks ASICs.

2. FPPS float

FPPS is insurance. Network snapshot: ~930.7 EH/s, subsidy 3.125 BTC, fee proxy 0.10 BTC, ~$79,110, 14-day horizon, 1% fee.

Hot wallet ≤ 2 days of liability. User seeds never land on pool boxes. Workbook is in the workspace at artifacts/dev/orion/ops/orion-fpps-float.xlsx.

3. Swap rails

THORChain or Chainflip as quote backends. Hosted instant-exchange APIs are a money-transmitter shape — reject as the core.

4. Vault libraries

Replace the demo wordlist with @scure/bip39 + @scure/bip32 before mainnet.

5. Legal posture

A pool that pays on-chain to miner-controlled addresses is a service. A hosted convert with balances and no KYC is the wrong object. Keep the desk as a router. Not legal advice.
